# K1ND.world v4.13.0 changelog

V4.13.0 turns the V4.12 context engine into a more accountable multimodal product preview. It preserves the 49-language, 259-rule/signals policy foundation and adds real local image-text evidence, rewrite-fidelity gating, appeals, persistent-target intelligence and language-quality reporting.

## Added

- On-device Apple Vision OCR for PNG, JPEG, WebP and GIF uploads, with immediate temporary-file deletion and SHA-256 evidence identity.
- Caption, whole-image text, individual text-region and fused caption+image moderation. The strongest evidenced result governs the whole post.
- Fail-closed media handling: unavailable or uncertain analysis routes to review instead of being declared safe.
- A live Media Evidence Lab with real clean, intervention and threat PNG fixtures.
- Uploads in the trusted-poster composer, including visible OCR evidence in intervention and block flows.
- Rewrite-fidelity evaluation for source recall, stance preservation and invented-content risk.
- Automatic suppression of safe-sounding rewrite candidates when meaning fidelity is not proven.
- Member appeals and a moderator adjudication queue with upheld, overturned and corrected outcomes.
- Corrected/overturned appeals become governed regression-learning items; browser policy never changes automatically.
- A cross-thread author→target harassment graph derived from recorded intervention context.
- A 49-group language-quality dashboard that separates functional fixture coverage from unmeasured pilot precision/recall.

## Honest boundary

OCR is active in this macOS preview. A production visual hate classifier, known-harm hashing and forensic manipulation model are not bundled and are explicitly marked unavailable. V4.13 does not claim universal image or multilingual moderation.
