# K1ND.world V4.12.0 changelog

## Cross-context moderation intelligence

- Resolves participant aliases, indirect player references and family references using conversation context.
- Adds persistent author/target behaviour evidence across threads, escalating severity and post-match burst detection.
- Keeps clean sporting criticism publishable despite earlier interventions.
- Adds inspectable policy, language, target, rewrite and uncertainty confidence fields.

## Language and evasion wave

- Adds 50 functional rules across Tamil/Tanglish, Telugu/Tenglish, Kannada/Kanglish, Malayalam/Manglish, Marathi, Nepali, Hausa, Yorùbá, Igbo and Nigerian Pidgin.
- Adds 70 native, transliterated, code-switched, severe, harm, dehumanisation and safe-context fixtures.
- Preserves non-Latin combining marks while folding Latin diacritics for Romanisation matching.
- Adds eight cross-context signals and 18 fixtures for code-switching, stacked labels, separator/repeated-letter evasion and ambiguity-aware coded-language review.

## Rewrite fidelity

- Generates direct and measured options from the source-backed proposition.
- Exposes clause-level retain/remove/qualify decisions.
- Re-moderates every option; only clean options are displayed or selectable.
- Continues to offer no automatic rewrite when a post contains abuse but no legitimate point.

## Demo and operations

- Demo Lab now shows 49-language coverage, six conversation scenarios, rewrite choices, coded-meaning evidence and confidence.
- Hate Stress Test adds a six-context simultaneous challenge console.
- Admin exposes 259 inspectable rules/signals with dedicated V4.12 filters.
- **814/814** combined canonical and adversarial checks pass.
