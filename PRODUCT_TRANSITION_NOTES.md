# K1ND.world — product transition notes

V4.24.1 is the Hosted Tournament Operations Deployment, built from the preserved V4.24.0 release. It retains the event-wide operating picture and adds a supported public pilot-demo path with a same-origin Python service, persistent evidence storage, private hosted credentials, HTTPS session controls and Linux QR rendering.

## Still preview-only

- The tournament map, court schedule, athletes, scores and rehearsal traffic are simulated.
- The event-wide comparison is a local counterfactual replay, not measured harm reduction or athlete wellbeing evidence.
- Incident preparation and authorisation are local workflow proof; no external connector can deliver a packet.
- The four-court rehearsal proves functional isolation and orchestration, not venue scale, concurrency or production resilience.

- The Arena match, score, identities and rehearsal traffic are simulated.
- A temporary event pass is not production identity or credentialing.
- The local QR defaults to a loopback URL; phone access requires explicit LAN binding and a trusted network.
- Server-sent events and timed synchronization are local demo transport, not production scale or latency evidence.
- The 40-participant cap is a local safety boundary, not tested venue capacity.

- Public Experience is a presentation curtain, not a production authorization boundary or separate deployment.
- The live match, score, accounts and message history are simulated.
- Match Room polling is a local demonstration, not a production realtime transport or latency claim.
- Personal shield preferences are local audited state, not a production athlete-entitlement or account-sanction system.
- The crowd-pressure score is an explainable routing heuristic, not a probability, attribution or forecast.
- The semantic reasoner is deterministic coverage for represented patterns, not a production language model.
- Demo PINs and the local registry are not production identity.
- SQLite is local preview persistence, not managed multi-host storage.
- Forty-nine functional language groups do not establish equivalent native-speaker field quality.
- No production multilingual context model is configured.
- No governing-body, venue-security, credentialing, location-intelligence or emergency-dispatch feed is connected.
- WTA, ATP, venue-security and emergency routes are non-live labels for governed drills.
- Restricted evidence is not stored in a production KMS-backed vault.
- Threat scoring is routing support, not probability, guilt or autonomous emergency judgement.
- Self-harm classification is content-safety routing, not clinical assessment.

## Required before a live tournament pilot

1. Contract named governing-body and venue-security endpoints with authentication, ownership, acknowledgement, timeout and fallback procedures.
2. Agree lawful disclosure, jurisdiction, safeguarding, emergency escalation and law-enforcement referral protocols.
3. Replace demo accounts with governed identity, recovery, MFA and privileged-access management.
4. Move persistence to encrypted managed infrastructure with migrations, backups, recovery, monitoring and audit export.
5. Implement a restricted evidence vault with KMS/HSM controls, retention, deletion, legal hold and break-glass review.
6. Staff a trained round-the-clock safeguarding function with welfare support and rehearsed escalation ownership.
7. Put maintained multilingual and specialist models behind the existing provider seams.
8. Evaluate priority languages with native communities and independently adjudicated representative traffic.
9. Measure harm-specific false negatives, false positives, time-to-triage, acknowledgement/fallback performance and operator workload.
10. Commission privacy, security, safeguarding, human-rights and adversarial reviews before live event processing.
