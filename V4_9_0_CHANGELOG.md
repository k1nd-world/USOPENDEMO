# K1ND.world v4.9.0 changelog

Build: **Conversation Intelligence Product Preview**

V4.9.0 was created from an exact copy of V4.8.0. The V4.8 source and archive remain unchanged.

## The six product additions

1. **Live conversation history** — feed comments and replies now send the real post author, reply target and recent public turns into moderation. The resulting evidence follows the decision into review and Trust Trail views.
2. **Participant-aware safeguards** — known professional athletes and U18 participants are identified. Athlete status informs risk without suppressing fair criticism; direct abuse of a known U18 participant receives enhanced review.
3. **Cumulative targeting detection** — recent interventions are assessed for repeat-author targeting, target shifts and multi-account pile-ons. A warning-level attack can be held when the developing conversation makes the risk materially higher.
4. **Governed language operations** — Admin now includes a language-candidate intake, searchable active-policy console and clear native-review/testing boundary. Browser submissions cannot become active rules automatically.
5. **Live red-team learning loop** — Demo Lab probes can be recorded, reviewed, promoted or dismissed. Hard-block originals remain withheld from the operational list.
6. **US Open presenter lock** — a single control hides role switching, resets and non-essential navigation for a cleaner live walkthrough.

## Moderation and language changes

- Added 24 functional rules across Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch.
- Added original-language rewrites for recoverable dismissive requests.
- Expanded context intelligence to eight recent turns and real participant profiles.
- Added standalone, athlete reply, developing pile-on and U18 Demo Lab scenarios.
- Expanded bare-insult handling so the engine refuses to invent a generic replacement when no genuine point exists.

## Product boundary

V4.9 is a functional browser product preview. It is not a production trust-and-safety service. Secure backend enforcement, authentication, persistent databases, production multilingual/visual models, specialist review operations, audit retention, appeals, monitoring and incident response remain production integrations.
